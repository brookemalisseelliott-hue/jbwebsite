Hardscape

Austin's hardscape contractor for backyards built for living.

Concrete patios, paver walkways, fire features, retaining walls, and full outdoor living hardscape across Austin and the Hill Country.

Standard & stamped concrete·Pavers, stone, & flagstone·Fire features & fireplaces

Hardscape is the foundation of any real outdoor living space. The patio you set the table on, the path you walk to the firepit, the wall that turns a slope into a usable yard. We install all of it: poured concrete, stamped and stained finishes, pavers, flagstone, retaining walls, and the fire features that make a backyard feel finished.

What we install

  • Concrete patios: standard pour, stamped, stained, broom-finished, or salt-finished.
  • Paver patios & walkways: brick, concrete pavers, and natural stone in any pattern.
  • Flagstone patios: for that natural Hill Country look.
  • Fire pits & outdoor fireplaces: wood-burning or gas, stone or stucco, freestanding or pavilion-integrated.
  • Retaining walls: block, stone, or poured concrete to turn slopes into usable backyard.
  • Outdoor kitchens & bar tops: concrete countertops, tile, or stone, plumbing and gas roughed in.
  • Cover-ready slabs: if you're planning a future patio cover or pergola, we pour with the footings sized right from day one.

Where we work

We do hardscape across Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Leander, Georgetown, Lakeway, Buda, Kyle, and Dripping Springs.
