Hardscape questions from Austin homeowners

How much does a concrete patio cost in Austin?

A standard concrete patio in Austin typically runs $8 to $15 per square foot installed. Stamped or stained concrete runs $15 to $25 per square foot. Final pricing depends on size, thickness, prep work, and any pour-in features like footings for cover posts.

Pavers or concrete patio, which is better?

Concrete is more affordable, faster to install, and lasts 30+ years if poured correctly. Pavers cost more upfront but offer easier individual repairs, more visual variety, and better drainage. We help you pick based on your yard, look preference, and budget.

How long does a concrete patio install take?

Most residential concrete patios are poured in 1 day, after 1 to 2 days of grading and forming. The concrete needs 5 to 7 days to fully cure before heavy use.

Can you build a fire pit or outdoor fireplace?

Yes. We build wood-burning and gas fire pits, full outdoor fireplaces with stone or stucco finishes, and pavilion-integrated fireplaces tied to patio covers. Every project is built to code with proper clearances.
