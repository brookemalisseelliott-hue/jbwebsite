Free roof inspections·Insurance claims handled·Written warranty

A roof in Austin doesn't just sit there. It bakes in 100-degree summers, takes hail in the spring, and wakes up to driving rain in the fall. Replacing it well, with the right materials and the right crew, is the difference between a 15-year roof and a 30-year one. That's what we do.

What we install

  • Architectural asphalt shingles: the standard for Austin homes. We install Class 4 impact-rated shingles wherever insurance discounts apply.
  • Standing-seam metal roofs: 40+ year lifespan, reflects heat, looks incredible on Hill Country homes.
  • Synthetic underlayment + ice-and-water shield: every roof gets the right base, not the cheapest one.
  • Storm-damage repair: hail, wind, fallen limb, leak. Free inspection, honest assessment.
  • Re-roofs & full tear-offs: we don't roof over old layers; we tear off, inspect the decking, and start fresh.
  • Gutters, ridge vents, and soffit/fascia work: the parts most contractors forget about.

Free roof inspections after Austin storms

If you've had hail or high winds (common any spring in central Texas), call us before you call your insurance company. We come out, climb the roof, and tell you what we see, free. If there's damage, we document it with photos and work directly with your adjuster so the scope reflects what's actually needed. If there's no real damage, we tell you that too.

Storm just hit? We'll inspect your roof for free and document everything in writing. Same-day callback when you reach out.

How a JB roofing project goes

  1. Free inspection. John climbs the roof, takes photos, walks you through what he sees.
  2. Line-itemed quote. Materials, labor, dump fees, permits, all spelled out. No surprises.
  3. Insurance coordination (if storm). We work with your adjuster directly so the scope reflects reality.
  4. Install day. Crew shows up, tarps your landscaping, tears off, decks check, underlayment, install. Most homes finish in 1 to 2 days.
  5. Walkthrough & warranty. Magnetic sweep for nails, final walkthrough, written warranty in your hand.
