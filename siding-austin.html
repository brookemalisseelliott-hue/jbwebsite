Siding questions from Austin homeowners

How much does Hardie siding cost in Austin?

Hardie fiber cement siding in Austin typically runs $9 to $14 per square foot installed, including tear-off, house wrap, trim, and paint. Larger or two-story homes, and homes with extensive trim work, run higher.

How long does a siding replacement take?

A full siding replacement on an average Austin home takes 1 to 3 weeks depending on size, prep work, and weather. Partial replacements (gable ends, single walls) typically wrap in 3 to 5 days.

Hardie vs. wood vs. vinyl, which is best for Austin?

Hardie fiber cement is the most popular siding in Austin for good reason: it handles heat, doesn't rot or warp, and holds paint for 15+ years. Real wood looks great but needs more maintenance. Vinyl is the cheapest but tends to warp in extreme Texas heat.

Will siding replacement increase my home value?

Yes. Fiber cement siding replacement is consistently one of the highest ROI exterior remodel projects, typically returning 65 to 80 percent of cost in resale value, plus a major curb appeal upgrade.
