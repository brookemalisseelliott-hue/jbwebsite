Siding

Austin's siding contractor for curb appeal that lasts.

Hardie fiber cement, wood, and engineered siding installs and replacements across Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, and the Hill Country.

Hardie certified·Full & partial replacements·Written warranty

Siding is the single biggest visual change you can make to a home, and one of the highest-ROI projects you can do. Done right, it transforms curb appeal, seals the house against weather, and pays back most of its cost at resale. Done wrong, it traps moisture, peels paint, and looks worse than what you started with. We do it right.

What we install

  • Hardie fiber cement siding: the Austin standard. Doesn't rot, doesn't warp, holds paint 15+ years, fire-resistant.
  • Engineered wood siding (LP SmartSide and similar): warm wood look with composite durability.
  • Cedar and real wood siding: for craftsman, modern farmhouse, and Hill Country builds.
  • Gable-end and partial replacements: if only part of the home needs siding work, we don't sell you a full re-side.
  • Trim, soffit, fascia, and house wrap: the parts most contractors cut corners on. We don't.
  • Repaints during reside: two-coat exterior paint with the right primer for whatever substrate is going on.

Where we work

We side homes across Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Leander, Georgetown, Lakeway, Buda, Kyle, and Dripping Springs.
