# Statute check of contract-template.md (18 Oct 2026)

Compared against official Texas text (statutes.capitol.texas.gov PDFs). Not legal advice; have a Texas construction attorney confirm before first real use.

| Notice | Statute rule | Template status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9A Chapter 27 defects | Prop. Code 27.007: "substantially similar", 10 pt bold | Matches the model text. OK. |
| 9B Insurance deductible | Bus. & Com. Code 27.02(b): exact text, 12 pt bold, contracts of $1,000 or more paid from a claim | Word for word. OK. |
| 10 Right-to-cancel statement | Bus. & Com. Code 601.052: bold, 10 pt, in immediate proximity to the customer signature | Word for word, repeated above signatures. OK. |
| 9D Disclosure statement | Prop. Code 53.255(b): "must read substantially similar to" the statutory statement | NEEDS CHANGE. The template is a plain-language summary that drops required content: "NEVER SIGN DOCUMENT THAT INCLUDES AN UNTRUE STATEMENT", "if you fail to comply with the terms of the documents, you could lose your property", the contractor's duty to furnish a disbursement statement with each payment request, the mechanic's lien affidavit explanation, and the lien-release and title-insurance detail. Recommendation: use the statutory text verbatim, with its headings, as the disclosure document. A plain-language summary can sit above it, labeled as a summary. |
| 10 Notice of cancellation form | Bus. & Com. Code 601.053(a): specific statements in 10 pt bold, in duplicate, with merchant name, address, transaction date and deadline date | NEEDS CHANGE. The template uses the federal FTC Cooling-Off Rule wording (16 CFR 429.1) in sentence case. Texas prescribes its own wording in 10 pt bold, and its goods paragraph differs ("IF YOU DO NOT AGREE TO RETURN THE GOODS TO THE MERCHANT OR IF THE MERCHANT DOES NOT PICK THEM UP WITHIN 20 DAYS..."). Recommendation: render the Texas 601.053 text verbatim in bold, with the email address added as an additional way to send "any other written notice". Ask the attorney whether to also include the FTC form. |

## Also worth confirming with the attorney

- Chapter 601 only applies to sales solicited away from the contractor's place of business (601.002). Giving every customer the 3-day right anyway is allowed and simpler, which is what the template does.
- "Business day" is not defined in Chapter 601. The template uses the FTC definition (every day except Sundays and federal holidays). Confirm.
- Section 53.255 disclosure must be delivered before the owner signs. The signing flow (separate acknowledgment checkbox before Sign) handles this.
- 811: removed from the contract by choice. Texas Utilities Code Chapter 251 still requires the excavator to call 811 before digging, so crews should keep doing it.
