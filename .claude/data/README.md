# Baseline data snapshot (saved 30 Sep 2026)

Pulled through a Supermetrics trial that ended 1 Oct 2026. Use these as the
"before" numbers when judging whether later SEO work moved anything.

Files: GSC query-by-page (90 days), GSC daily Sept, GA4 weekly sessions,
real leads by month, Google Business Profile monthly actions and search terms.

## The numbers that matter

- Non-brand Google searches, 90 days to 29 Sep: 6,156 impressions, 2 clicks.
  Brand searches (people typing the business name): 1,170 impressions, 18 clicks.
- Weekly Google impressions: 939 (Sep 1-7) to 1,210 (Sep 22-28), +79%.
  Clicks flat at roughly one a day all month.
- Near page one with zero clicks: Buda deck terms 464 impressions at 7 to 12;
  Cedar Park pergola terms 378 at 8 to 17; Round Rock 261 at 6 to 19.
- Website sessions: summer average about 19 a week; September about 30 to 55
  real sessions a week (the week of Sep 15 read 102 but was inflated internally).
- Real leads: form 2 / 1 / 5 in Jul / Aug / Sep; site phone taps 0 / 0 / 5.
- Google Business Profile: found almost only by name ("jb contractors"). Calls
  from the profile 3 / 1 / 9 / 1 in Jun to Sep. 25 lifetime reviews at 5.0.

## Data hygiene notes

- GA4 property that receives the site is "brooke" (456845050); the two
  properties named after the website are empty.
- Analytics only fires on the live domain and never when the URL has ?notrack.
