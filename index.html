Meet John

Hi, I'm John. I built this business on honest work.

JB Contracting is a mom-and-pop operation built on hard work, honesty, and treating customers like family. When you hire us, you're not just getting a contractor. You're getting a neighbor who genuinely cares how your home turns out.

I've spent decades in this trade, from small home repairs to full roof replacements to backyard transformations the whole neighborhood notices. The thing I'll never compromise on is transparency. You'll always know what's happening with your project, what it costs, and why. No surprises, no upcharges, no games. Just honest work I'd be proud to put my name on.

How It Works

Simple, transparent, no surprises.

From the first call to the final walkthrough, here's exactly what working with us looks like.

Tell Us Your Vision

Use the Design Builder, give us a call, or text us photos of your space. We'll get back to you within 24 hours and set a time to come see it in person.

Free On-Site Estimate

We come out, walk the project with you, and write up an honest, line-itemed quote. No pressure, no hidden fees, no obligation to book.

Build Day

Our crew shows up on time and gets to work. John texts you daily updates with photos so you always know exactly where the project stands.

Walkthrough & Warranty

We finish, clean up after ourselves, and walk every detail with you. Every job is backed by warranty. If something isn't right, we make it right.

Our Story

Why we do this.

My wife Brooke and I built JB Contracting together, the same way we're building our life. With our hands, with care, and with the people we love at the center of every decision.

Every deck, every roof, every fence we build is somebody's home. Somebody's Sunday morning coffee spot. Somebody's place where their kids will grow up. I never forget that. When I finish a project, I want it to be the kind of work I'd be proud to do on my own house, because the family living there matters as much as mine does.

That's the whole reason this business exists. Not to be the biggest contractor in Austin. Just to be the one your family trusts enough to call back the next time.

John & Brooke
