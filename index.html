Our Story

Why we do this.

My wife Brooke and I built JB Contracting together, the same way we're building our life. With our hands, with care, and with the people we love at the center of every decision.

Every deck, every roof, every fence we build is somebody's home. Somebody's Sunday morning coffee spot. Somebody's place where their kids will grow up. I never forget that. When I finish a project, I want it to be the kind of work I'd be proud to do on my own house, because the family living there matters as much as mine does.

That's the whole reason this business exists. Not to be the biggest contractor in Austin. Just to be the one your family trusts enough to call back the next time.

John & Brooke
