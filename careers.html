Careers · JB Contracting ATX

We are always interested in good people.

Small crew, greater Austin, honest work. Tell John what you do and he will keep you in mind.

Experienced trades·John reads every one·Subs welcome

JB is a small crew and it stays small on purpose. What we need most is people who show up when they said they would, tell the truth about what they know, and care whether the work is right.

The work

Outside, in Central Texas. Early starts because of the heat. Lumber, concrete, ladders, uneven ground, and driving between jobs across greater Austin. It is also genuinely satisfying: you finish a thing, you can see it, and the person who owns it is usually standing right there being pleased about it.

What we look for

  • You show up when you said you would. This is most of it.
  • You tell the truth about what you know. Nobody minds "I have not done that before." Everybody minds finding out later.
  • You care whether it is right, including the joint nobody can see from the patio.
  • You are safe, and decent to be around. Small crew, and somebody else's yard with their kids in it.

No resume needed

Plenty of good tradespeople have never written one and it says nothing about whether they can build. Tell us what you have worked on and that is enough.

Subcontractors welcome too, particularly concrete crews and roofers.

We cannot promise there is an opening today. John reads every one of these himself, and calls when there is.
