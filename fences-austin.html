What we build

  • Cedar privacy fences: Western Red Cedar, 6 or 8 foot. Naturally rot-resistant, no chemicals, ages beautifully.
  • Horizontal slat fences: the modern Austin look. Boards run sideways for clean, contemporary lines.
  • Board-on-board (vertical privacy): the classic. Overlapping pickets eliminate gaps as the wood shrinks.
  • Shadowbox fences: identical from both sides, semi-private with airflow. Great compromise between privacy and breeze.
  • Split-rail and ranch fences: Hill Country style for acreage and rural lots.
  • Custom gates: matching pedestrian gates, double drive gates, and side gates with self-closing hardware.

Common questions

How much does a cedar privacy fence cost in Austin?

A 6-foot Western Red Cedar privacy fence in Austin typically runs $45 to $75 per linear foot installed. Pressure-treated runs $28 to $48 per linear foot. Final pricing depends on terrain, posts (steel vs. wood), gate count, and stain options. Every quote we provide is line-itemed and free.

How long does a fence install take?

Most residential fence projects in Austin are completed in 2 to 4 days. Larger acreage installs and split-rail Hill Country fences can take 1 to 2 weeks.

Cedar or pressure-treated for an Austin fence?

Cedar lasts 20 to 30 years in Austin and looks better untreated. Pressure-treated lasts 15 to 20 years and is roughly 30 percent cheaper. Most Austin homeowners choose cedar if they can fit it in budget. We help you compare based on your yard size and look preference.

Do I need a permit for a fence in Austin?

Most residential fences in Austin under 7 feet do not require a permit, but HOA approval and easement checks are common. We handle the easement check and HOA submission as part of every project.
