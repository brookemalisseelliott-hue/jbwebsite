Fences

Austin's cedar fence builder for privacy that lasts.

Cedar privacy fences, horizontal slat builds, custom gates, and split-rail Hill Country fences. Built right, the first time, across greater Austin.

Free on-site estimates·HOA submissions handled·Written warranty

A fence is the kind of project that's "small" until you start pricing it out. The difference between a five-year fence and a thirty-year fence is mostly the choices you make in the first hour. We help you make the right ones.

What we build

  • Cedar privacy fences: Western Red Cedar, 6 or 8 foot. Naturally rot-resistant, no chemicals, ages beautifully.
  • Horizontal slat fences: the modern Austin look. Boards run sideways for clean, contemporary lines.
  • Board-on-board (vertical privacy): the classic. Overlapping pickets eliminate gaps as the wood shrinks.
  • Shadowbox fences: identical from both sides, semi-private with airflow. Great compromise between privacy and breeze.
  • Split-rail and ranch fences: Hill Country style for acreage and rural lots.
  • Custom gates: matching pedestrian gates, double drive gates, and side gates with self-closing hardware.
  • Stain & sealing services: if you want it sealed on day one, we do that too.

Where we work

We install fences across Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Leander, Georgetown, Lakeway, Buda, Kyle, and Dripping Springs, and the rest of Travis, Hays, and Williamson counties.
