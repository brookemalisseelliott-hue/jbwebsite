Pergolas & Patio Covers

Austin's pergola builder for shade that looks good.

Custom cedar pergolas, attached patio covers, and outdoor living structures across Austin and the Hill Country. Beat the Texas heat in style.

Cedar & metal options·Lighting & fans wired in·Permits handled

Austin summers are long. The right pergola or patio cover turns your backyard from a place you avoid in July into the spot you spend the whole evening. We build them right: real lumber, real hardware, real foundations, and the ceiling finishes that make a cover look like an extension of the home, not a tacked-on afterthought.

What we build

  • Freestanding cedar pergolas: the classic look. Open beams for partial shade, perfect for string lights and outdoor dining.
  • Attached patio covers: solid roof structures tied into the home. Tongue-and-groove cedar ceilings, recessed lighting, ceiling fan ready.
  • Lean-to covers: shed-style roof off the back of the house. Affordable way to extend covered outdoor space.
  • Pavilion-style structures: larger covered outdoor rooms with stone fireplaces, outdoor kitchens, and built-in seating.
  • Pergolas with lattice walls or polycarbonate roofs: for areas where you want light through but rain blocked.
  • Lighting, fans, and speakers wired in: we run the electrical inside the framing during the build, not surface-mounted later.

Where we work

We build pergolas and patio covers across Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Leander, Georgetown, Lakeway, Buda, Kyle, and Dripping Springs.
