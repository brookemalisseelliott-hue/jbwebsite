Pergola questions from Austin homeowners

How much does a pergola cost in Austin?

A standard 12x12 cedar pergola in Austin typically runs $6,000 to $12,000 installed. Larger pergolas, attached patio covers with roofing, and built-in lighting or fans run $12,000 to $30,000 depending on size and finishes.

Pergola or patio cover, what's the difference?

A pergola has open beams overhead, providing partial shade and air flow. A patio cover has a solid roof (shingle, metal, or polycarbonate) that fully blocks sun and rain. Many Austin homeowners pick a patio cover for full Texas-summer protection, or a pergola for a softer, more open look.

How long does a pergola install take?

Most freestanding cedar pergolas are completed in 4 to 7 days. Attached patio covers with electrical for lighting, fans, or speakers usually run 1 to 2 weeks.

Do pergolas need permits in Austin?

Most pergolas under 200 square feet do not require a building permit, but attached patio covers and any structure with electrical typically do. We pull every permit needed as part of the project.
