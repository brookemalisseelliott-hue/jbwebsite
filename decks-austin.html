A deck should make your backyard feel finished. It should be somewhere your family actually wants to spend a Saturday. We've been building decks across the greater Austin area for years, and we've learned that the best ones come from getting the basics right: good materials, careful framing, real craftsmanship, and honest pricing.

What we build

  • Pressure-treated pine decks: the workhorse. Affordable, durable, and great for big footprints. Lasts 15 to 20 years in Austin's climate.
  • Western Red Cedar decks: warmer color, naturally rot-resistant, no chemicals. Our most popular upgrade.
  • Composite and PVC decking: Trex, TimberTech, and similar. Lowest maintenance, longest lifespan, premium look.
  • Multi-level decks, wraparound porches, and elevated decks: Hill Country slopes are no problem.
  • Built-in benches, planters, lighting, and pergola integrations: built right into the framing, not bolted on after.
  • Stairs, landings, and railings: code-compliant, comfortable rise and run, pickets done right.
