Austin deck questions, answered

How much does a new deck cost in Austin?

Pressure-treated decks in Austin typically start around $30 to $45 per square foot installed, cedar runs $50 to $75 per square foot, and composite materials run $60 to $100 per square foot. Final pricing depends on height, railings, stairs, site access, and whether you want built-ins like benches, planters, or lighting. We measure the space and hand you a line-itemed quote so you can see exactly what each piece costs.

How long does it take to build a deck in Austin?

Most residential decks take 1 to 3 weeks of build time once permits are pulled. Smaller pressure-treated decks can wrap in under a week. Larger multi-level cedar or composite builds with built-in lighting or benches take longer. You get a firm timeline at the estimate, and John texts daily progress photos while the crew is on site.

Do I need a permit to build a deck in Austin?

In most cases, yes. The City of Austin generally requires a permit for any deck attached to a home or higher than 30 inches off the ground. We handle the permit process as part of every project we build, including the HOA architectural review submission when your neighborhood requires one.

What's the best decking material for Austin's climate?

For Austin's heat, sun, and occasional storms, the three best choices are pressure-treated pine (most affordable, 15 to 20 year lifespan), Western Red Cedar (warmest look, naturally rot-resistant, 20 to 30 years with upkeep), and composite or PVC (lowest maintenance, 25 to 50 years, no staining). Cedar is our most popular upgrade. Composite makes the most sense if you never want to re-stain.
