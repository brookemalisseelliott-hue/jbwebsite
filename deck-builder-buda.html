Custom deck installation in Buda

Every deck we build in Buda starts with the same three questions: what the yard does when it rains, how much sun the back of the house takes in July, and how you actually plan to use the space. The answers change the framing, not just the finish.

  • Cedar decks. Western Red Cedar is the Buda favorite: it handles our heat, takes stain beautifully, and stays cooler underfoot than composite in August. Roughly $50 to $75 per square foot installed.
  • Pressure-treated pine decks. The value build. Same framing standards, same hardware, a lower material cost. Roughly $30 to $45 per square foot installed.
  • Composite and PVC decks. If you never want to stain again, this is the one. Roughly $60 to $100 per square foot installed.
  • Covered decks and patio builds. A deck plus an attached cover or pergola, framed as one structure so the posts carry the roof properly instead of being bolted on later.
  • Floating and low-profile decks. Common on the flatter Sunfield and Whispering Hollow lots where a raised deck would sit awkwardly against the slab.
  • Deck tear-out and replacement. We haul the old one, check the footings, and rebuild. Most rotted Buda decks fail at the ledger and the posts first, not the boards you can see.

What deck installation in Buda actually involves

  1. Free on-site estimate. John walks the yard, measures, and talks through materials and layout. You get a written, line-itemed quote, not a number scribbled on a card.
  2. HOA and permits. Most Buda subdivisions require ARC approval before a deck goes in. We prepare the submission packet and pull permits with the City of Buda or Hays County when the build needs them.
  3. Footings and framing. The part nobody sees and everything depends on. Proper footing depth for our clay soil, correct ledger flashing, and hardware rated for treated lumber.
  4. Decking, railings, and finish. Boards, railings, stairs, skirting, and stain or sealer.
  5. Cleanup and walkthrough. We haul everything off and walk the finished deck with you before we call it done.

Most Buda deck builds run one to three weeks depending on size, cover, and whether we're tearing out an old structure first. You get progress photos by text every day it's worked on.
