Cedar Park is full of homes built in the late 90s and 2000s, and a lot of them went up with builder-grade masonite, hardboard, or early vinyl on the elevations the street doesn't see. Twenty-odd years of Texas sun later, that siding is chalking, swelling at the bottom edge, or quietly letting water into the sheathing behind it. Replacing it is most of the siding work we do in Cedar Park.

Installation, replacement, or repair?

Three different jobs get called siding installation in Cedar Park, and they price nowhere near each other, so it is worth knowing which one you are asking about before you collect quotes.

New installation is siding going onto bare sheathing: an addition, a rebuilt wall, a converted garage, a new screened porch or sunroom that needs its exterior finished. There is no tear-off, no disposal, and no unpleasant surprise waiting behind the old material, so it is the cleanest and cheapest of the three per square foot.

Replacement is the bulk of what we do here. The old hardboard, masonite or vinyl comes off, the sheathing gets inspected and repaired, the house gets re-wrapped, and new fiber cement goes on. Most of the cost difference against a new install is tear-off, disposal and whatever the wall is hiding.

Repair is a section, a course, or a corner. Sprinkler damage at the bottom of one wall. A hail strike on the west elevation. A woodpecker. If the rest of the house is sound, a repair is genuinely the right answer, and we will say so even though it is the smallest invoice on the list.

Why fiber cement, specifically here

Central Texas is unusually hard on exterior cladding. Long stretches of direct UV, wild swings in humidity, hail most springs, and a termite population that takes wood seriously. Fiber cement is the material that handles all four at once.

  • It does not rot or swell. The failure mode of hardboard and masonite here is water wicking into the bottom edge. Fiber cement has no organic material to absorb it.
  • Termites have no interest in it. Meaningful in Williamson County.
  • It holds paint far longer. Factory-finished boards routinely go 15 years between repaints, against 5 to 7 for wood siding in this sun.
  • It handles hail far better than vinyl, which cracks, or aluminium, which dents permanently.
  • Insurers and buyers both recognise it. It reads as a genuine upgrade at resale, not a repair.

Signs your Cedar Park siding is finished

Homeowners usually call us about the one wall that looks bad. Half the time the wall that looks worst is not the wall with the problem. Things worth walking out and checking yourself, ideally on a sunny morning when shadows show texture:

  • Swelling or crumbling at the bottom course. Especially anywhere a sprinkler head reaches. On hardboard this is the classic Cedar Park failure and it means water has been sitting there for years.
  • Chalk on your hand. Run a palm down a sunny elevation. A white film is the paint binder giving up, which is a repaint at best and past saving at worst.
  • Soft spots. Press firmly around windows and at the corners. Anything that gives is wet behind the surface.
  • Gaps at the butt joints. Boards that have shrunk apart are letting water directly onto the wrap, and the wrap is twenty years old too.
  • Paint failing again within two or three years. Paint does not fail that fast in isolation. It fails that fast because the substrate is holding moisture.
  • Interior clues. A stain in the top corner of a bedroom, or an outlet on an exterior wall that smells musty, usually starts outside.
  • Dimpling or cracking after a hail spring. Vinyl cracks outright. Hardboard bruises. Both are claimable and both have deadlines.

Permits, HOA review, and Cedar Park specifics

A straight like-for-like re-side in Cedar Park generally does not need a building permit. The moment structural sheathing is replaced, an opening is altered, or the work is part of an addition, it does, and it is pulled through the City of Cedar Park. Homes just outside the city limits fall under Williamson County instead, which is a different counter and a different timeline. We sort out which one you are in before we quote, because getting that wrong is how a two-week job becomes a two-month job.

HOA architectural review is the part that actually delays people here. Review commonly takes two to four weeks. We prepare and submit the packet, including colour samples and elevation photos, and we start it the day you sign rather than the week before we are due on site.

Things worth knowing before you get quotes

Ask what happens when they find rot

On a house with twenty-year-old hardboard, some bad sheathing is likely. A quote that says nothing about it either has not thought about it or is planning to renegotiate once the walls are open. Get the repair rate in writing before you sign.

Clearance at grade matters

Fiber cement needs a specified gap between the bottom course and the ground or a hard surface. Installed tight to the dirt it will wick moisture, and it will also void the manufacturer warranty. It is one of the most common installation faults we see on re-sides done cheaply.

Factory finish versus site paint

ColorPlus arrives finished, has a long finish warranty, and only needs touch-up at the cut ends. Site painting gives you any colour you like and costs less up front, but you are relying on the paint job rather than a factory coating. Both are legitimate. Know which one you are being quoted.

If it is hail, involve your insurer first

Cedar Park takes hail most springs, and siding damage is frequently covered alongside the roof. Do not let anyone start work before the adjuster has seen it. We do free inspections and meet the adjuster on site.

Cedar Park siding installation questions, answered

How much does siding cost in Cedar Park, TX?

James Hardie fiber cement siding in Cedar Park typically runs $10 to $16 per square foot installed, including tear-off, house wrap, trim, and paint. A single elevation usually lands between $6,000 and $14,000. A full re-side runs about $17,000 to $26,000 on a 1,500-square-foot single storey, $22,000 to $34,000 at 2,000 square feet, and $30,000 to $44,000 on a 2,500-square-foot two storey. Rotted sheathing discovered during tear-off is the main variable, so we quote that repair rate up front.

Do I need to replace all my siding or just part of it?

Often just part. On a lot of Cedar Park homes only the west and south elevations have really failed, because those take the sun. If the rest is sound, replacing one or two elevations can buy you another decade for a fraction of the cost. The catch is matching, so if you are changing colour anyway a full re-side often makes more sense. We will tell you honestly which situation you are in at the inspection.

How long does a siding job take in Cedar Park?

Most Cedar Park re-sides run one to three weeks on site, depending on the size of the house and how much sheathing has to be replaced once the old material comes off. Single-elevation jobs are usually under a week. HOA colour approval can add time before we start, so it is worth submitting early.

Will my insurance cover storm-damaged siding?

Frequently, yes. Cedar Park gets hail most springs, and siding damage is often covered alongside roof damage on the same claim. Do not have any work started before your adjuster has inspected it. We do free damage inspections, document what we find, and meet the adjuster at the house.

Is Hardie siding worth it over vinyl in Central Texas?

In this climate, we think so. Vinyl cracks under hail and can distort in sustained heat, and neither is unusual here. Fiber cement will not rot, does not interest termites, holds paint far longer under our UV, and handles hail much better. It costs more up front and it is the material we would put on our own house in Williamson County.

Do you handle Cedar Park HOA approval for a colour change?

Yes. Most Cedar Park subdivisions require architectural review for exterior colour changes, and several maintain an approved palette. We prepare and submit the packet as part of the project, including colour samples where the HOA wants them.

Do you install siding on new construction and additions?

Yes. New installation onto bare sheathing is a straightforward job and it prices better per square foot than a replacement, because there is no tear-off, no disposal, and nothing hidden behind the old material. We regularly finish additions, rebuilt walls, converted garages, and the exteriors of new screened porches and sunrooms in Cedar Park.

Can you install new siding over the existing siding?

It can be done and we do not recommend it. Going over the old material hides the exact thing the inspection is meant to find, which is whether the sheathing underneath is wet or rotted. It also adds thickness that throws off window and door trim depth. On a Cedar Park home with twenty-year-old hardboard, there is usually something back there worth knowing about.

What is the best siding for a Cedar Park home?

Fiber cement, in nearly every case. Cedar Park gives cladding long stretches of direct UV, hail most springs, big humidity swings and an active termite population, and fiber cement is the one common material that handles all four. LP SmartSide engineered wood is a legitimate second choice at a lower price. Vinyl is the cheapest quote you will receive and the one we talk homeowners out of.
