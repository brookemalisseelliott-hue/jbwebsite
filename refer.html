For Our Customers · Referral Thank-You

Know someone who needs a good contractor?

If we have built something for you, the kindest thing you can do is tell a neighbor. When their project goes ahead, we will send you a $250 gift card as a thank-you.

$250

A $250 gift card, as a thank-you, for every friend, neighbor or family member you send our way whose project goes ahead.

Almost all of our work comes from people who have seen a job we did, or heard about one from someone they trust. If we built something for you and you were happy with it, the best thing you can do for a small, family-owned business is tell someone. This is our way of saying thank you when you do.

How it works

  1. Send us their details with the form below, or just text John at (512) 587-3914 with their name and number. Let them know we will be in touch.
  2. John calls them, walks the project and gives them a free, line-itemed estimate. No pressure and no obligation.
  3. If they go ahead, you get a $250 gift card once their job is finished and paid for.

Fences, decks, pergolas, patio covers, roofing, siding and concrete, anywhere across Travis, Hays and Williamson counties.

Questions about referrals

When do I get the gift card?

When the job you referred is finished and paid for. We will text you when it is on its way.

Is there a limit?

No. Refer three neighbors who each go ahead and you get three gift cards.

What if my friend just wants a quote?

That is completely fine. John will still walk the project and give them a free, line-itemed estimate with no pressure. The thank-you only applies if they go ahead.

Do I have to be a past customer?

No. If you know someone who needs a fence, deck, pergola, roof or siding, send them our way.
